ABOUT SWIFTVISA

Founded in Hyderabad, 2008. Trusted by 10,000+ Students.

Swift Visa is an overseas education consultancy with one job: getting you into the right course, at the right university, for the right reasons. Over 12+ years we've earned the trust of more than 10,000 students with honest, profile-first guidance.

Our study abroad visa services cover the USA, UK, Canada, and Australia. Course shortlisting, applications, scholarships, and student visa work are all free for students. Universities pay us, so you don't.

Our Vision
